HOW TO PLAY

      

        
You are a farm boy come to Regal City to seek fortune in Hornbluff's Arena — The Pit.


        
Regal City

        
    
          
  • Weapons Shop — buy hand weapons, ranged weapons, armor, and ammo. Sell old gear.
    • 
          
  • Healer — restore HP or buy healing potions (use them in combat).
    • 
          
  • Tavern / Magic / Gambling — risk gold for rumors, potions, or luck.
    • 
          
  • Bank (+ / −) — deposit gold before you fight. If you die, carried gold is looted.
    • 
          
  • The Pit (Arena) — pick an opponent tier and enter combat.
    • 
        


        
Arena combat

        
    
          
  • You are Ω. Reach the door D to flee (you forfeit, but live).
    • 
          
  • Arrow keys / WASD — move. Each phase you get limited moves and attacks.
    • 
          
  • A — melee attack (must stand next to the foe, then choose a direction).
    • 
          
  • F — fire ranged weapon (straight line of sight, needs projectiles; too close = blocked).
    • 
          
  • B — beg for mercy (only before you attack; some foes may spare you).
    • 
          
  • P — drink a potion. . — end your phase (foe acts).
    • 
        


        
Progression

        
    
          
  • Wins give gold and score; score raises your level and attacks per phase.
    • 
          
  • You have a limited number of fights per day. Death ends the day — you return poorer tomorrow.
    • 
          
  • Progress autosaves in the browser. Use Continue on the title screen to resume.
    • 
        


        
Tip: start on Trainees, bank your gold often, and flee if a fight goes south.
