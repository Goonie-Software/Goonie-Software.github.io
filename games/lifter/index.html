Adventure RPG

Lifter

In Lifter, you step into the shoes of a cunning yet kind-hearted thief trying to save their home from eviction. Set in a sprawling world teeming with secrets, this adventure RPG weaves a tale of desperation, cleverness, and moral choices.

Every job presents unique challenges — stealth, agility, and wit. Unlock gadgets and skills to outsmart store employees, disable security, and disappear into the shadows. As the eviction date approaches, the pressure mounts. Survival isn’t just about what you steal — it’s about what you stand for.

Key features

  • Dynamic heist mechanics — plan and execute jobs with stealth, strategy, and environmental puzzles.
  • Moral choices that shape the city and your character’s story.
  • A vibrant world with colorful NPCs and hidden paths.
  • Customizable tools and playstyles for your approach to thievery.

Will you become the legend who stole back their future, or will the world swallow you whole? The choice is yours in Lifter.
